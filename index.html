Frequently asked questions

Which machines do you support?

JCB, Volvo and Caterpillar excavators and loaders, graders, dozers, piling rigs, road equipment and sugar cane harvesters.

Can you diagnose a fault remotely?

Often yes. Send the model, symptoms, any fault codes and photos or short videos. Some faults need a physical inspection, and we will tell you when that is the case.

Do you travel for on-site support?

Yes. We support fleets in India, Fiji and the UAE. Send your site location and we will confirm availability.

What should I send with my request?

Machine make and model, year, operating hours, what the machine is doing, when it started, any fault codes, and recent repairs.

Are you an authorised dealer?

No. We are an independent service provider with dealer-level experience, not affiliated with the manufacturers.
